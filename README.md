Welcome to my personal developer portfolio! This website showcases my journey as a developer, my technical skills, selected projects, and my passion for building modern, interactive, and user-friendly digital experiences.

## 🌐 About the Project

This portfolio is designed to present my development skills, highlight projects I have worked on, and provide an easy way for visitors, recruiters, and potential clients to connect with me.

## ✨ Features

- **Modern UI/UX:** Clean, futuristic design with a dark theme.
- **Interactive Hero Section:** Animated text showcasing my development specialties.
- **Project Showcase:** Interactive project presentations with animated transitions.
- **Responsive Design:** Designed to work across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **Smooth Animations:** Dynamic visual effects and scroll-based interactions.

- **Matrix-Inspired Background:** Subtle animated digital effects.
- **Social and Project Links:** Easy access to my work and professional profiles.
